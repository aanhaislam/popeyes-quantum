DICKE QUEST

Exact preparation of Dicke states on Google's Willow chip using only CZ on calibrated couplers and PhasedXZ single qubit gates. This page collects the mathematics behind each construction and what it buys in gate count and depth.

1The target state

The Dicke state on N qubits with n excitations is the equal superposition of every bitstring of Hamming weight n.

|Dn⟩N=(Nn)−1/2∑x∈{0,1}N,|x|=n|x⟩ EVERY ALLOWED PATTERN HAS PROBABILITY 1 / C(N,n)

It is symmetric under every permutation of qubits, and the complement gives a free reduction to the lighter case.

|Dn⟩N=X⊗N|DN−n⟩Nk=min(n,N−n) ONLY k ≤ N/2 IS EVER BUILT

n = 1 is the W state. Permutation symmetry also means any SWAP at the end of a circuit can be deleted, since relabelling qubits leaves the state unchanged.

IMBALANCE ERASES THAT

If string b appears with probability (1 + δb)/K, the χ² statistic gains a noncentral part, and it can no longer land near 0, so the lucky draws disappear.

λ=104fδ2¯ 1% IMBALANCE · λ ≈ 0.9 · K = 2 SCORE 51.4 → 33.3 · 3% IMBALANCE · λ ≈ 8 · SCORE 1.3

So the order of priorities is a valid circuit for every case, then balance within about 1% on the small K cases with correct signs, then a high fraction of valid shots, and only then large N. Amplitudes cannot be tilted to cancel a known bias, because the noiseless state must be exact. Balance has to come from the construction and from choosing good qubits.

3Split and cyclic shift

Conditioning on the last qubit gives the recursion that the Bärtschi and Eidenbenz construction runs backwards.

|Dn⟩N=nN|Dn−1⟩N−1⊗|1⟩+N−nN|Dn⟩N−1⊗|0⟩

Starting from |0…01…1⟩ with k ones, step ℓ moves a share of the excitation one position along a path. Block i of that step is a rotation controlled on the neighbouring qubits.

θℓ,i=2arccosiℓ,i=1,…,k

Every gate acts on neighbours along a path, so the circuit maps straight onto a chain of coupled qubits with no routing. The cost is linear in N for fixed k, and depth is linear too, which is what the later sections attack.

4Exact building blocks

Single qubit gates merge into one PhasedXZ between consecutive CZs, so only CZs count. Every block below is checked numerically against the isometry it defines.

KNOWN TARGET ROTATION · 1 CZ

When the target is guaranteed to be |0⟩ whenever the control is |1⟩, one CZ replaces the usual two.

Ry(φ)ZRy(−φ)=Ry(2φ)Z,Z|0⟩=|0⟩

EXCITATION TRANSFER · 2 CZ, PROVEN

|10⟩ ↦ √(1−p)|10⟩ + √p|01⟩ with |00⟩ fixed. With one CZ, keeping |00⟩ a product forces the first layer on the source to be diagonal, and then |10⟩ also stays a product. So two CZs are necessary.

MOVING HOLE G₃ · 5 CZ

CNOT, a multiplexed Ry on the middle qubit, CNOT. An exhaustive numerical search found no 3 or 4 CZ circuit on a line.

M=Ry(α)ZcRy(β)ZbRy(−β)ZcRy(−α)α=θ+π4,β=θ−π4TOTAL ANGLES 0, 0, θ, π FOR (c,b) = 00, 10, 01, 11

MERGED TOP BLOCK · 4 CZ, NOT 7

The first two sweep steps of a shift merge into one block. An exhaustive search over CZ orderings found that only one works, and it was then derived analytically.

CZabCZbcCZabCZbc

A shift step therefore costs 2 CZ for k = 1, 4 for k = 2 and 4 + 5(k − 2) in general, all on nearest neighbours.

WHOLE STATE SYNTHESIS · N ≤ 8

Dicke states are real, and {Ry, CZ} is universal for real states. The ansatz is Ry on every qubit between brickwork CZ layers on a path or a 2 × m ladder. Exact gradient L-BFGS drives the infidelity below 10⁻¹³, then greedy pruning deletes CZs while the circuit stays exact. Removing one more CZ from any deployed circuit leaves infidelity of at least 3 × 10⁻³.

5W states in logarithmic depth

For n = 1 the excitation starts on a root and spreads along a spanning tree. A node owning the excitation for mv qubits passes it to child c with probability s(c)/mv, where s(c) is the size of the child's subtree.

θv→c=2arcsins(c)mv

The probability of reaching any qubit is a product along its root path that telescopes to 1/N, and every amplitude stays real and positive, so the result is exactly |WN⟩. The first transfer acts on a product state and needs one CZ.

#CZ=1+2(N−2)=2N−3,depth≈4⌈log2N⌉+1 EXHAUSTIVE SEARCH CONFIRMS W₃ NEEDS 3 AND W₄ NEEDS 5, SO 2N − 3 IS OPTIMAL THERE

A line needs N − 1 rounds. For W10 the tree takes 15 time steps against 37, and under idle noise the X term drops from 0.0075 to 0.0042. Every branch carries exactly one excitation at all times, so energy decay lowers every valid string equally and creates no imbalance.

6Two arm split

Split the qubits into a left arm of a and a right arm of b = N − a. The number of excitations on the right arm follows the hypergeometric law.

|Dk(N)⟩=∑jP(j)|Dk−j(a)⟩L|Dj(b)⟩R,P(j)=(bj)(ak−j)(Nk)

A thermometer chain writes Σj √P(j) |1j0k−j⟩ on the right arm with k − 1 CZ. The left arm is laid out as a reversed ladder, so one parallel layer of k CNOTs copies the complement. Both arms then run the shift recursion at the same time.

7Choosing qubits under noise

Every calibrated rate enters as reported value plus one standard uncertainty, which hedges against qubits that only look good by chance. Placement tries all 8 grid symmetries and every translation onto calibrated qubits and couplers, and minimises

cost=∑CZecz+∑1Qesq+∑qεq+∑qtq(1T1+1Tφ)+log(1+λroK−1)
εq=(1−p)e0→1+pe1→0,p=nN EACH QUBIT READS 1 WITH PROBABILITY n/N IN A DICKE STATE

λro is the χ² excess predicted from the spread of readout asymmetry e1 − e0 across the chosen qubits. A string whose ones sit on leaky qubits is read correctly less often, and a random choice of qubits gives about 0.6% imbalance from readout alone. Pre compensating amplitudes would break exactness, so qubit choice is the only remedy.

ROBUST ANGLES AND ECHOES

One CZ structure has many exact angle sets. The deployed set has the least worst case regret over a range of Ry over rotation, which cuts sensitivity to 1% over rotation by 1.1 to 5.5 times. Idle windows with at least 4 free single qubit slots get X Y X Y, whose product is −I, so the ideal state is unchanged.

8What the hardware checks

Each case is scored from one 10 000 shot run per basis. With K = C(N,n) valid strings and f the fraction of shots with exactly n ones,

z=χ2Kf,x=∑w=1M(cw−cwideal)2(cwideal)2,score=1z+x c_w = AVERAGE WEIGHT w CORRELATOR IN THE X BASIS

Even a perfect state has a shot noise ceiling, with median score about K / median(χ²K−1), and every result is reported as a fraction of it. For larger n, x grows as large as z and CZ count is the lever.

10Open directions

COMMUTATION SCHEDULING · TESTED, NOT MERGED

CZ is diagonal, so any two CZs commute, and so do CZ and Z rotations. In a W split the child holds its final amplitude after its second rotation, so its next CZ can run before the parent's last one.

[CZpc,CZcd]=0,[CZpc,Rz(α)c]=0

A list scheduler that orders only non commuting operations cuts W depth by 1 or 2 moments for most N. On this version of the code it raises the bonus from 37.15 to 37.82, with every circuit still exact.

CERTIFYING ENTANGLEMENT

A Dicke state has total spin j = N/2 and Jz = n − N/2, so its transverse spin is as large as it can be.

⟨Jx2+Jy2⟩=N2(N2+1)−(n−N2)2

Large transverse spin with small variance of Jz is out of reach for states with only a few qubits entangled. The witnesses of Tóth and of Lücke et al. turn measured values into a lower bound on entanglement depth. ⟨Jx2⟩ comes from the X basis run, and a strict bound also needs ⟨Jy2⟩ from one extra basis.
